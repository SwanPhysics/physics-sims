General College Physics — Simulations

Interactive demonstrations for PHY 201 & 202 · Instructor:John Swanlund

Ch 3 · §3.4

Projectile Motion

Change launch angle, speed and height; see the trajectory, velocity components, range and maximum height.

 Ch 5 · §5.1

Inclined Plane with Friction

Find when a block starts to slide (tan θ > μs) and how its acceleration depends on the angle and μk, with a free-body diagram and position–time graph.

 Ch 6 · §6.1–6.3

Uniform Circular Motion

Set speed, radius and mass; time the rotations and see the velocity, centripetal acceleration and centripetal force vectors.

 Ch 12 · §12.7

Osmosis and Osmotic Pressure

Watch water cross a membrane until back pressure balances osmotic pressure; change molecule size, concentration and pore size, or push a piston for reverse osmosis.

 Ch 13 · §13.1

Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics

Put two potatoes in thermal contact, watch energy flow until they reach equilibrium, and use a thermometer to show the zeroth law.

 Ch 13 · §13.4

Kinetic Theory of Gases

Measure gas pressure from molecular collisions, compare it with PV = NkT, and see the Maxwell–Boltzmann speed distribution.
