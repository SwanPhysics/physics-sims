Heat Conduction at the Molecular Level

OpenStax College Physics 2e, Section 14.5

Five conducting strips and heat graph

  • Particle shade: cold (blue) → hot (red); the numbers are in the readout table
  • Small dots in copper: free electrons
  • Ring and trail: one followed particle per strip
  • Diamond: stiff bonds between light atoms in a perfect crystal; vibrations travel as waves (phonons) and scatter rarely.
  • Copper: free electrons race through the lattice of ions, carrying energy and trading it with the ions when they collide.
  • Glass: strong bonds but a random network; vibrations scatter within an atom or two, so energy hops slowly from neighbor to neighbor.
  • Water: heavier molecules packed close but free to move; energy passes by jostling collisions. (Real water would also carry heat by convection; that is switched off here.)
  • Air: a few molecules far apart; energy moves only when a molecule crosses the gap or collides.
