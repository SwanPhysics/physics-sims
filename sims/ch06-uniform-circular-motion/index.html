Calculated values are hidden. Measure the period yourself.

Motion runs in real time, counterclockwise. The view zooms to fit the circle; use the 1 m grid and the 2 m scale bar to judge size. Arrow lengths grow with each value (each kind of vector has its own scale); inward arrows are shortened if they would pass the center. As with any real turntable, each rotation takes very slightly more or less time than 2πr/v.

Try this

  1. At v = 4.0 m/s and r = 5.0 m, time 5 rotations. Find the period and your measured speed. How close is it to 4.0 m/s?
  2. Repeat the same measurement three times. Why don't you get exactly the same time? List two sources of the differences.
  3. Double the speed at the same radius. What happens to the period? To the centripetal acceleration?
  4. Double the radius at the same speed. What happens to the period and to ac?
  5. Double the mass. Which quantities change, and which stay the same?
  6. Turn on all three vectors. Which point toward the center? Which point along the path? Why is there no force along the path?
